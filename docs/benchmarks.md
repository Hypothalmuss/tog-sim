## Notes

- P1, throughput levers (2026-08-22): at this layout the cell is tray-supply bound. A tray's pockets are placeable for
  about 5 s of its passage and trays arrive every 11 s (0.55 m pitch; the trays ride the 0.06 m/s outfeed at 0.050 m/s,
  ground truth - the belt slips), so the arm idles 1.6–2.0 s per
  cycle whatever the motion does. A faster profile (P1d) or a slower outfeed (P1a) keep picks/min inside the baseline
  CI; a tighter pitch (P1b) overloads the simulator (real-time factor drops and the look-ahead, then in wall seconds,
  aimed ahead of the products); leaving at unseal (P1c) costs precision. 13–14 picks/min at 100 % success is this
  cell's number; more needs a second tray lane or a denser tray, not a faster arm.
- Look-ahead since 2026-08-23: the pick/place leads are scaled by the measured real-time factor (`rtf` in the cycle
  lines), so a loaded simulator no longer produces no-seal picks.
- P3, bar tray (2026-08-23): bars on `tray_bar_2x3` went from 9–27 mm mean with trays shoved by the arm to 4.4 mm mean /
  6.1 mm p95, 12/12, 0 shoves (bar-only run, 30 bars/min). Five causes, each confirmed against ground truth before
  its fix: the tracker fused suction points that jump ±20 mm along a bar between frames (now it tracks the product
  centre); the blended transfer arrived ~20 mm short in z and a bar clipped the near wall (transfer now 50 mm above
  the walls); 6 mm of pocket side clearance against a ~10 mm spread (pocket 190 × 62); the tray-pose visibility gate
  counted every product in the image and the lattice gates were below the bar error (fixed in the vacancy node);
  0.62 m trays spawned 0.55 m apart (the spawner now keeps the pitch ≥ the tray lengths). Mask/box centre
  estimators alone did not help: the error was consistency between the grasp point and the centre, not the estimate.
- P5, precision levers (2026-08-23): the carton benches after the perception changes had drifted to 3.7 / 8.0 mm with a
  −9 mm tail along the belt while the at-release errors were unchanged: the place goal ended 0.08 s after contact but
  the product lets go 0.1–0.35 s after the release command, so the cup stood still while the tray moved on with the
  product still attached. `release_dwell_s` 0.25 (the cup keeps tracking the pocket through the unseal) brings the
  2×40 bench to 3.2 / 6.2 mm at the same picks/min (the cell is supply-bound, the extra dwell lands in idle) and is
  the default. `track_lag_s` 0.04 on top (aim ahead by belt speed × servo lag: the along-belt bias went −2.8 → −0.8 mm)
  brings the 2×40 bench to 2.7 [2.4–3.0] / 5.6 [4.6–6.5] mm - the baseline precision with the whole bar-tray stack -
  and is the default too. Its row's lower wall-clock picks/min is the machine load (rtf 0.58 in run 1, see the column).
