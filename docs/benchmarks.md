## Notes

- P1, throughput levers (2026-08-22): at this layout the cell is tray-supply bound. A tray's pockets are placeable for
  about 5 s of its passage and trays arrive every 11 s (0.55 m pitch; the trays ride the 0.06 m/s outfeed at 0.050 m/s,
  ground truth - the belt slips), so the arm idles 1.6–2.0 s per
  cycle whatever the motion does. A faster profile (P1d) or a slower outfeed (P1a) keep picks/min inside the baseline
  CI; a tighter pitch (P1b) overloads the simulator (real-time factor drops and the look-ahead, then in wall seconds,
  aimed ahead of the products); leaving at unseal (P1c) costs precision. 13–14 picks/min at 100 % success is this
  cell's number; more needs a second tray lane or a denser tray, not a faster arm.
- Look-ahead since 2026-08-23: the pick/place leads are scaled by the measured real-time factor (`rtf` in the cycle
  lines), so a loaded simulator no longer produces no-seal picks.
