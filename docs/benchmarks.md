Reading: at this cell layout the throughput is bounded by the tray supply — a tray is placeable for ~5 s of its passage
through the reach zone and trays arrive every 9.2 s — so faster motion (P1d) or a slower outfeed (P1a) do not move
picks/min outside the baseline's confidence interval, and a tighter tray pitch (P1b) overloads the simulation. Success
and placement precision are unchanged across all variants. Raising the rate further needs a layout change (outfeed
closer to the robot / a second lane), not more software tuning.
